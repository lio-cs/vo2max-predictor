# AeroCoach — Disclaimer draft (for legal/compliance review)

**Status: draft only.** This is a starting point to make the actual legal/compliance read
faster, not a substitute for one — nothing here should be treated as legal sign-off. Flagging
this explicitly since the checklist item this responds to exists precisely because a real
review is still needed before any of this goes in front of real users.

---

## Why this needs a real review, not just careful wording

Two things push this into more careful territory than a generic wellness app disclaimer:

1. **STOP-BANG screens for a specific, named disease (OSA).** FDA's General Wellness Policy
   safe-harbor (which exempts most "helps you sleep better"-style apps from medical device
   regulation) generally does **not** cover products that claim to detect/screen for a specific
   disease — it's built for general wellness claims, not disease-specific ones. The mitigating
   factor here: AeroCoach implements the *standard, published, already-validated* STOP-BANG
   scoring exactly as documented (not a novel derived algorithm), doesn't diagnose, and
   explicitly routes people to a real clinician for anything beyond low risk. That's consistent
   with how other consumer-facing STOP-BANG calculators operate without being regulated as
   medical devices — but "consistent with how others do it" is not the same as "cleared by our
   own counsel," and this is exactly the kind of judgment call a real reviewer should make, not
   an AI assistant.
2. **The explanatory text is LLM-generated.** The system prompt constrains Gemini to a plain-
   English translation layer and never lets it change the risk tier or action (both are
   deterministic, see `lib/geminiCoach.ts`) — but the *wording* of each explanation is still
   model output, not fixed copy. A disclaimer needs to hold regardless of the specific phrasing
   Gemini produces on any given run, not just for the phrasing we happened to test.

## Draft: short version (for the UI, near the STOP-BANG form/results)

> STOP-BANG is a validated screening questionnaire, not a diagnostic test. AeroCoach does not
> diagnose obstructive sleep apnea or any other condition — only a clinician-ordered sleep
> study can do that. The information shown, including any AI-generated explanation, is general
> health information, not medical advice, and should not delay seeking care if you're concerned
> about your symptoms.

## Draft: full version (for a dedicated disclaimer/terms section)

> **Medical disclaimer**
>
> AeroCoach is a practice/educational build and is not a medical device. It does not diagnose,
> treat, cure, or prevent any disease, including obstructive sleep apnea (OSA).
>
> The STOP-BANG questionnaire used in this app is a published, independently validated
> screening tool (Chung et al.) used as-is, with its standard scoring thresholds. A screening
> tool estimates *likelihood*, not presence, of a condition — it is not a substitute for
> clinical evaluation. Only a clinician-ordered sleep study (polysomnography) can diagnose OSA.
>
> Explanatory text, rationale, and encouragement shown alongside your results are generated by
> an AI language model (Google Gemini). While the underlying risk tier and recommended action
> are determined by fixed, deterministic rules — not the AI — the specific wording is
> AI-generated and may vary between uses. Review any output for accuracy before relying on it,
> and treat it as general information, not personalized medical advice.
>
> VO2max and fitness-trend information shown alongside your OSA screening result is separate,
> general health context. It is not evidence of OSA risk specifically and is not used to
> compute your STOP-BANG score or risk tier.
>
> If you are experiencing symptoms that concern you — including loud snoring, witnessed
> breathing pauses, or excessive daytime sleepiness — talk to a healthcare provider regardless
> of what this tool shows. Do not use this app in place of professional medical evaluation,
> diagnosis, or treatment. If you think you may be experiencing a medical emergency, call
> emergency services immediately.

## Open questions for the actual reviewer

- Does the "used a validated tool as-is, doesn't diagnose, routes to a clinician" framing hold
  up under whatever jurisdiction(s) AeroGlyphics actually operates/sells in — this draft assumes
  a US FDA/FTC framing since that's what the team's own research (`AEROCOACH_PLAN.md` §7) has
  been grounded in so far.
- Does emphasizing that explanatory text is AI-generated create its own disclosure obligation
  (some jurisdictions are starting to require AI-content labeling) beyond what's drafted above?
- Are the specific stats used elsewhere in marketing copy (83.7M US adults, 80% undiagnosed,
  etc. — already fact-checked as real and sourced, see `AEROCOACH_PLAN.md` §7) sufficiently
  substantiated/cited wherever they're actually displayed to satisfy FTC truth-in-advertising
  expectations, not just accurate in the source research?
- Should this disclaimer live inline (as drafted) or gate access (e.g. a one-time acknowledgment
  before first use)? Purely a product/legal call, not something resolved here.
